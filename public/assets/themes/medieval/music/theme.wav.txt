ASSET: theme.wav
THEME: medieval
FOLDER: /assets/themes/medieval/music/

DESCRIPTION
  The single background music track for the entire medieval theme.
  Plays on the login screen and continues uninterrupted into the home screen.
  Fades out (1.5 s) when the learner navigates deeper (quest, library, shop,
  social) and fades back in (0.8 s) when they return to login or home.

  Suggested style:
    - Opens gently (suits the login gate atmosphere), builds slightly
      for the home hub feeling — ideally one continuous piece that
      works in both contexts.
    - Celtic/fantasy: lute, flute, light harp, soft drum
    - 80–100 BPM, mood: mysterious at start, warm and lively by the loop

TECHNICAL
  Format  : WAV (MP3 also works — update the src extension in musicStore.ts)
  Loop    : Yes — seamless loop. Match tail to head to avoid gap.
  Volume  : Mix for ~35% system volume (store sets audio.volume = 0.35)
  Duration: 90–180 s recommended before the loop point
  Channels: Stereo

NOTES
  Drop the file here as theme.wav.
  The ♪ / ♩ button appears in the bottom-right corner on login and in the
  header bar on home once the audio is ready to play.
  If this file is absent the music system silently does nothing.
