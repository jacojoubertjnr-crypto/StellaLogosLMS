# Theme Asset Placeholder - Replace with Pixel Art

This is a placeholder for ambient sprites (castle, trees, etc.).
Replace with a 16-bit pixel art PNG file.

Recommended: Seamless landscape elements like:
- Medieval castle/tower
- Trees or vegetation
- Ambient scenery
- Size: 512x640px or larger (will be positioned on sides)

These will be positioned absolutely on the left and right edges
and appear to be part of the background landscape.
