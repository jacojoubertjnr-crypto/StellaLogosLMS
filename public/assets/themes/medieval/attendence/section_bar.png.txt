Page: AttendenceUI [The Tavern] — SmartContainer collapsed bars

Purpose: Background for the three 52px-tall collapsed section bars:
         "TODAY'S TIMETABLE", "CLASS ROSTER", and "CLASS CHAT".
         Each bar is clickable and expands to a full-frame overlay.
         All three bars share this single texture asset.
Recommended Size: 860x52px
Format: PNG-32 with transparency

Style: A thick stone slab or iron-banded wooden bar — like a tavern counter edge or
       castle door lintel. Slightly raised or embossed feel. The ▶ chevron sits on the
       right side; the section title and preview text sit on the left. Dark enough for
       gold text to read clearly. Small decorative studs or iron rivets at the ends optional.

Notes:
  - background-size: 100% 100% stretches it to fill the bar at any width
  - Left zone ~400px: title + preview text
  - Right ~32px: ▶ expand indicator
  - The bar brightens its gold border on hover — the texture itself does not change
  - All three section bars use the same asset, so it must read well for
    "TIMETABLE", "ROSTER", and "CHAT" labels equally
