## STELLA LOGOS SYSTEM BRIEFCustomization Shop & Economy Specifications (Version 4)
1. Executive Summary & PurposeThe Stella Logos Customization Shop is a game-inspired rewards module designed to boost student engagement and motivation. Learners acquire points through consistent academic effort, timeliness, high-quality submissions, and significant term-over-term improvement. These points are then redeemed for highly visual interface enhancements.The storefront functions as a low-maintenance, static but expandable catalog, ensuring maximum operational simplicity for administrative staff while sustaining long-term interest through item releases over the course of the year.2. Visual Continuity & System CohesionTo ensure a seamless user experience, the visual identity of the Shop interface must maintain absolute alignment with existing design themes. Key styling rules include:Structural Theme Sync: All shop typography, spacing, navigation items, and overall UI style must automatically inherit the rules and CSS variables established on other pages.Theme Influence Boundaries: Themes alter backgrounds, container visual elements, panel styles, and borders. Themes do not alter font families, font colors, or typography styles, preserving readability and global system typography settings.3. Points Generation Model (Annual Basis)The economy is tuned around a standard 35-week academic calendar, targeting exactly 10,000 points by the end of the year for top-tier learners.Attendance: 25 pts per class (up to 3,500 pts annually)Reliability: 40 pts per on-time submission (up to 1,400 pts annually)Quality: Up to 100 pts for marks above 80% (up to 3,500 pts annually)Engagement: 10 pts per day for daily active platform interaction (up to 1,400 pts annually)Term Growth: 25 pts per 1% increase in term-to-term overall marksExam Comeback: 50 pts per 1% increase from June to November exam marks5.
