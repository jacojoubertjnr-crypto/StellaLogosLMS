
## Project Brief: Stella Logos Landing Page
This document serves as a technical and creative blueprint for a high-impact, single-page landing page inspired by the Blizzard Entertainment aesthetic: dark, immersive, and media-rich.

System Logic & Architecture
The website is designed for dynamic folder-based deployment. The build system should parse a root directory where:

Folder Names: Act as programmatic hooks for section IDs and navigation titles (e.g., 01_Hero, 02_About).

Folder Content: Markdown files (.md) for text, images (.png/.jpg), and video links (.mp4/YouTube URLs) will automatically populate the corresponding section.

Design Aesthetics
Visual Style: Cinematic and "Hero-centric." Use large-scale backgrounds, high-contrast typography, and smooth scroll transitions.

The Blizzard Influence: Use a sticky top navigation bar with a "glassmorphism" effect. Content blocks should feel like "cards" layered over thematic, 16-bit or high-fantasy environmental backgrounds.

Interactive Elements: Hover states on buttons should have a subtle glow; video placeholders should have custom "play" icons consistent with the Stella Logos branding.

Section-by-Section Content & Layout
1. [Folder: 01_Hero]
Layout: Full-screen immersive video or high-res thematic background.

Headline: "Welcome to Stella Logos."

Sub-headline: "The Home of the Highest Quality Online Learning."

CTA Buttons: [Sign Up Today] (Directs to Pricing) | [Sign In] (Directs to LMS Login).

The Themes Pane: A scrolling "Recent Themes" gallery showcasing 16-bit pixel art landscapes, medieval scrolls, and dynamic environmental assets.

2. [Folder: 02_About]
Headline: "Who is Stella Logos?"

Content: > Created by educators, for educators. Stella Logos is a collaboration between Professor Johannes Slabbert and Dr. Jaco Joubert.

Professor Slabbert, an Alumni Professor at the University of Pretoria, pioneered the "Facilitating Learning" philosophy. Dr. Joubert, a veteran teacher and school administrator, has extrapolated these tenets into a digital ecosystem.

Visuals: Side-by-side portraits with a clean narrative transitioning from "The Boardroom" to the "Digital Frontier."

3. [Folder: 03_Philosophy]
Headline: "Our Will. No Middlemen."

Content: > We removed the politics, the stakeholders, and the corporate filters. Every pixel and every pedagogical decision in Stella Logos is a direct reflection of our vision.

The Hidden Curriculum: > We intentionally design for the "hidden curriculum." Our platform fosters meta-learning and cooperative virtues, strengthening the intra- and interpersonal skills necessary for learners to thrive in a modern world.

4. [Folder: 04_Capabilities]
Headline: "A Facilitated Experience."

Facilitated Navigation: > Unlike "big menu" systems that leave learners lost, Stella Logos mimics the face-to-face classroom. Every click is a facilitated click, reducing cognitive load and guiding the learner every step of the way.

Authentic Gamification: > Designed by a lifelong gamer (from the 386 era to modern MMOs), our philosophy mimics the engagement of titles like League of Legends. Learners earn points for work to spend in a Thematic Shop for items that directly affect their learning interface.

Visuals: Embedded video loops showing the "LMS in action," engagement testers in videos, and the "Reward Shop."

5. [Folder: 05_News]
Headline: "Latest Results & Updates"

Content: A dynamic feed for the latest YouTube updates, messages from Dr. Joubert, or new feature announcements. This section ensures the site feels "alive" and evolving.

6. [Folder: 06_Pricing] (ID: signup-section)
Headline: "Start Your Journey."

Layout: Clear pricing cards leading to the registration portal.

Footer: Standard navigation links, contact info, and "Stella Logos" branding.

Navigation Tags (Anchor Links)
The header will feature the following links, with "Sign Up" styled as a high-contrast button:
About | Philosophy | Features | News | Pricing | [Sign Up]

The "Why" and "Where" of the Teacher Services Page

A separate page allows you to deep-dive into the training outcomes and create a mini-marketplace for your content.

Integrating into the Main Page Design:

I suggest replacing or updating one of the primary navigation anchor links (like "Pricing Options") and placing it strategically in the content flow. In our design, we can use the main sticky header (inspired by Blizzard's logic) to host a permanent "Services" or "Teacher Hub" link that always remains visible as users scroll.

This page should look identical to the main landing page (same cinematic backgrounds, glowing blue/lime accents, card-based layout, and polished typography) to keep the branding consistent.
